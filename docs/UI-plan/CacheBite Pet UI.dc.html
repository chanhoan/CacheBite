§6 · pet mascot (idle)

The pet — cat & corgi

The overlay renders one pet: whichever provider is primary (cat → Claude, corgi → Codex). The pet eats kibble — a stand-in for tokens. The more it eats, the fatter and lazier it gets, so its body reflects pet_mood = max(session_sev, weekly_sev): slim & lively at ok, chubby at warn, fat & sluggish at critical, a stuffed food-coma at exhausted. Each frame below is a drag-and-drop asset slot — drop your finished GIF per state and it flows into the rings and state gallery automatically.
